Description

Ukrainian varenyky are soft, handmade dumplings that are considered one of Ukraine's most iconic and comforting dishes. They consist of a thin, tender dough wrapped around a flavorful filling, then boiled until cooked. While potato is one of the most popular fillings, varenyky can also be filled with cheese, mushrooms, cabbage, meat, or even sweet ingredients like cherries or blueberries.

Potato varenyky have a mild, creamy filling that pairs beautifully with rich toppings such as melted butter, caramelized onions, and sour cream. The dough is delicate and slightly chewy, creating a satisfying contrast with the smooth mashed potato filling.

Varenyky have long been a staple of Ukrainian home cooking and are often prepared for family gatherings, holidays, and celebrations. Making them is traditionally a shared activity, with family members gathering around the table to roll out the dough, add the filling, and seal each dumpling by hand. Because of this, the dish is associated with warmth, hospitality, and togetherness as much as it is with its delicious flavor.

Simple, hearty, and versatile, varenyky are a classic example of Ukrainian comfort food that has been enjoyed for generations and remains popular both in Ukraine and in Ukrainian communities around the world.

Ingredients

    For the dough

  • 3 cups (375 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • ¾ cup (180 ml) warm water
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil

    • For the potato filling

  • 1½ pounds (700 g) potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 1 medium onion, finely diced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Optional: ½ cup shredded cheddar or farmer's cheese for a richer filling

    • For serving

  • Sour cream
  • Melted butter
  • Fried onions
  • Fresh chopped dill or parsley

Steps

  1. Make the filling. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.Add the potatoes and cook for 15–20 minutes, until tender.While the potatoes cook, melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook for 8–10 minutes until golden and caramelized. Drain the potatoes and mash until smooth. Stir in the cooked onions, salt, pepper, and cheese (if using). Let the filling cool completely.
  2. Prepare the dough. In a large bowl, combine the flour and salt. Beat together the egg, warm water, and vegetable oil. Gradually add the wet ingredients to the flour. Mix until a dough forms. Knead on a lightly floured surface for about 8–10 minutes, until smooth and elastic. Cover with a towel or plastic wrap and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Shape the dumplings. Divide the dough into two portions. Roll one portion to about ⅛ inch (3 mm) thick. Cut circles about 3 inches (7–8 cm) across using a glass or cookie cutter. Place about 1 tablespoon of filling in the center of each circle. Fold the dough over to make a half-moon shape. Press the edges firmly to seal. You can crimp the edges with a fork if desired.
  4. Cook the dumplings. Bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil. Add the dumplings in batches so they don't overcrowd the pot. Stir gently to prevent sticking. Once they float to the surface, cook for another 2–3 minutes. Remove with a slotted spoon.
  5. Serve. Serve the hot varenyky with:A generous spoonful of sour cream; Melted butter; Crispy fried onions; Fresh dill or parsley
